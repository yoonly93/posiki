How to follow your eye drop schedule after PRK

Jul 29, 2026

PRK drops may continue after the first uncomfortable days. Tracking start dates, end dates and taper days helps you stay aligned with the clinic’s instructions.

Medication after PRK varies. Do not change the frequency or duration of any drop without checking with your clinic.

PRK schedules can last beyond the uncomfortable days

After PRK, the early days get most of the attention, but eye drops may continue after the worst discomfort improves. Antibiotic drops, steroid drops, and artificial tears may each have a different duration.

PRK instructions are not automatically the same as LASIK instructions. If a taper is written for a steroid drop such as Pred Forte, mark the exact day the count changes.

Track the taper, not just the first week

  • Start and end dates for every bottle
  • Dose changes by week or by follow-up visit
  • Extra artificial tears used during the day

If your eye feels worse, your vision changes, or you are unsure whether to continue, ask your clinic instead of changing the schedule yourself.
