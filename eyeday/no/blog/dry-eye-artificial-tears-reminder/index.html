Dry eye and artificial tears

Do artificial tears need reminders too?

Jul 27, 2026

Artificial tears feel like the casual option, but if dry eye keeps coming back, simply recording when and how often you use them tells you a lot about your own patterns.

Instructions for artificial tears can differ by product and by whether they contain preservatives. If discomfort persists or gets worse, see an eye doctor.

Are you only reaching for them once it already hurts?

People with dry eye tend to look for artificial tears when their eyes feel gritty or stinging. Using them when symptoms appear does help, but if you spend the day in front of a screen, it is also worth getting ahead of the discomfort.

It is especially easy to miss a dose while you are concentrating at work. If your eyes only start to feel heavy and blurry in the afternoon, setting a reminder for earlier in the day can help.

A record reveals your patterns

  • Whether dryness is worse in the afternoon than the morning
  • Whether you use more drops on days you wear contact lenses
  • Whether symptoms flare after short sleep or indoor heating

That record helps you adjust your own habits, and it gives you something concrete to describe at your appointment instead of a vague impression.

Adding artificial tears in EyeDay

EyeDay is not only for prescription drops — you can add something you reach for repeatedly, like artificial tears. Log them as an extra dose whenever you use them, or set reminders at fixed times to see the pattern.
