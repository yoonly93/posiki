GIDS VOOR OOGDRUPPELS

Praktische manieren om
uw oogdruppels bij te houden

Het voorschrift is belangrijk, maar de juiste tijden en intervallen volhouden is vaak het lastigst. De EyeDay-blog behandelt veelvoorkomende vragen over herinneringen, doseringsregistratie en oogdrukmetingen.

*Het soort oogdruppels en de frequentie verschillen per kliniek en situatie. Dit is algemene informatie; volg altijd de instructies van uw behandelende kliniek.
Eye drops after cataract surgery

How to keep your eye drop schedule straight after cataract surgery

Cataract surgery does not end when you leave the operating room. Using the prescribed drops correctly for the weeks that follow is a real part of recovery.

Dry eye and artificial tears

Do artificial tears need reminders too?

Artificial tears feel like the casual option, but if dry eye keeps coming back, simply recording when and how often you use them tells you a lot about your own patterns.

The five-minute interval

Why multiple eye drops need a five-minute gap

When you are prescribed two or three drops, it is tempting to put them all in at once and be done. But different drops usually come with instructions to space them out.

Eye drop reminder apps

What to look for in an eye drop reminder app

A plain alarm app can get you to the right time. But once you have several drops, or you are managing them for months, it is worth checking whether an app was actually built for eye drops.

Glaucoma drop reminders

Why should glaucoma drops go in at the same time every day?

Glaucoma needs steady management even when you barely feel anything. With drops in particular, the habit that matters is using them at the prescribed times rather than whenever they come to mind.

Eye drop apps worldwide

Eye drop reminder apps and medication apps compared

Search for an eye drop reminder and you get eye-drop-specific apps, general medication reminders and your phone's built-in health app all at once. They are good at different things, so what to pick depends on whether your prescription is simple or complicated.

LASIK and LASEK drops

Why eye drop reminders matter after LASIK and LASEK

After vision correction surgery, using the prescribed drops consistently until your eyes settle is the part that counts. The first few days in particular can feel like a complicated schedule.

Multiple drop schedules

How to build a schedule when you have two or three drops

With one drop, a single alarm can be enough. Once there are two or more, times, order, left and right eye and duration all mix together and mistakes creep in.

Helping a parent

What to check when the family helps with a parent's eye drops

When a parent is on long-term glaucoma drops, or came home from cataract surgery with several bottles, having the family check in is often enough on its own to cut down on mistakes.

Steroid eye drops

What to watch when using steroid eye drops after cataract surgery

Steroid drops are often prescribed after cataract surgery. What matters is less the name of the drug than following the count and duration your clinic gave you.

Glaucoma drug names

How not to forget timolol and latanoprost drops

Glaucoma drops come with a generic name, a brand name and a bottle shape that all differ, which makes them feel complicated from the patient's side. What matters is using the drug you were prescribed, the way you were told, consistently.
